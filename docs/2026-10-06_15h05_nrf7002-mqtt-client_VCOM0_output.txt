IMPORTANT NOTES:
----------------
After a long debugging session with help of MS Copilot because the app connected to WiFi, disconnected and connected to WiFi again
with various error messages.
The source of the problem was in the files:
/boards/nrf54lm20dk_nrf54lm20b_cpuapp.conf 
and /root/prj.conf.
In both files this line has to contain "=n" instead of "=y": CONFIG_L2_WIFI_CONNECTIVITY_AUTO_CONNECT=n    
Then in /root/prj.conf, this line also has to contain "=n": CONFIG_NET_CONFIG_AUTO_INIT=n
This last line prevents that the zephyr system tries to automatically establish a WiFi connection at initialization.
